Being a genius just means you get a front-row seat to your own psychological collapse.
Franz Kafka spent his entire life writing thousands of pages about how terrifying and omnipresent authority is, all because his dad was loud at the dinner table. The lesson here is that you will never be good enough, so you might as well give up and become an insect.

The government can fake your execution, send you to a frozen wasteland in Siberia, and ruin your health, and you can still turn that entire experience into a 900-page book to tell people that they are not suffering enough.
